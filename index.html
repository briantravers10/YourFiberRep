Real Life Impact

What fiber means for your home.

Fiber doesn't just mean faster Netflix. Here's where you'll actually feel the difference day to day.

📷

Home Security Cameras

Security cameras constantly upload footage to the cloud. With DSL's weak upload speeds, cameras buffer, drop frames, or fail to record. Fiber's equal upload speed keeps every camera running smoothly, 24/7.

💻

Working From Home

Video calls, large file uploads, and cloud platforms all depend on upload speed. Poor upload = frozen calls and slow transfers. Fiber gives you the upload speed your job demands.

🎮

Online Gaming

Gaming isn't just about speed — it's about latency. High latency means lag and lost games. Fiber's low latency gives you a real competitive edge.

📺

4K Streaming & Smart TVs

4K streaming uses up to 25 Mbps per screen. With multiple TVs and devices, cable internet struggles. Fiber handles every screen in the house without breaking a sweat.

🏠

Smart Home Devices

Smart doorbells, thermostats, voice assistants, and more all need a reliable connection. Fiber supports dozens of devices simultaneously without slowdowns.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Multiple Users at Once

When everyone's home — streaming, gaming, on calls — cable internet can't keep up. Fiber splits bandwidth evenly so nobody slows anyone else down.

What's Happening Nationally

The US is going fiber — fast.

The government has invested billions to replace old copper infrastructure with fiber across the country. Here's what's happening right now.

Fiber Broadband Association

Record 10.3 Million US Homes Passed with Fiber in 2024

Fiber deployments hit a new annual record in 2024. There are now 88.1 million US homes passed with fiber, with the 2025–2029 period expected to see a 50%+ increase in homes reached.

Read the full report →
NTIA.gov — US Government

$42.45 Billion Federal Investment to Connect Every American

The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law's BEAD program is the single largest federal broadband investment in US history — designed to bring high-speed internet to every unserved and underserved community in America.

Read on NTIA.gov →
NetPMD — January 2025

Fiber Now Passes More Than 50% of US Households

As of 2024, fiber broadband has reached a critical milestone, passing over half of all US homes. In 2023 alone, nine million new homes were connected — a 13% year-over-year increase.

Read the full article →
Nation On Demand — 2026

BEAD Program: The Nationwide Broadband Transformation Has Begun

By 2025, most states completed their planning phases and are now awarding grants and beginning the first wave of fiber builds. This marks the true beginning of America's national broadband transformation.
